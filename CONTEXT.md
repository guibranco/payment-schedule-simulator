# Payment Schedule Simulator

A tool for inspecting, simulating and amending insurance payment schedules — the planned instalments that collect a policy's premium over its cover period — and checking them against what was actually collected.

## Language

### Schedule

**Schedule Item**:
One planned instalment in a payment schedule, with a period, due date and amount due.
_Avoid_: Collection, line, instalment record

**Collection Type**:
Whether a Schedule Item collects a whole period (Full) or the remainder of one after a change (Pro-Rata). These are the only two.
_Avoid_: Item type

**Full Item**:
A Schedule Item with Collection Type Full.
_Avoid_: Full collection

**Pro-Rata Item**:
A Schedule Item that adjusts what is collected for part of an already-collected period, following an MTA or Frequency Switch; its amount derives from its Basis Item and may be negative (a Refund Item). Always has a Basis Item.
_Avoid_: Pro rata collection, partial item

**Buffer Period**:
The month collected in advance, together with the running month, by a Monthly schedule's first Full Item; that first item also absorbs the Rounding Remainder.
_Avoid_: In-advance period

**Rounding Remainder**:
The cents left over when a premium is divided into 12 monthly instalments (at sale, MTA or any change), added to the first Full Item.

**Basis Item**:
The full-period item a Pro-Rata Item's amount is calculated from; usually generated by the Payment Schedule Service for the calculation rather than stored as a Schedule Item of the schedule. Only one level deep: a Basis Item never has a Basis Item of its own.
_Avoid_: Original item (except when naming the raw `originalItem` field)

**Refund Item**:
A Pro-Rata Item with a negative amount due, produced by an MTA that lowers the premium or by a cancellation. A derived category, not a Collection Type.

**Adjustment Date**:
When the Payment Schedule Service calculator last produced or touched a Schedule Item, e.g. adding an admin fee or calculating a Pro-Rata Item during an MTA; used for ordering and audit. Empty for items from the schedule's original calculation.
_Avoid_: Change date, modified date

**Stamp Duty (SMD)**:
A government levy charged as an admin fee (code SMD, €1 fixed so far), required on every schedule. Charges and refunds of it alternate: a live schedule always nets exactly one charge, a Voided one nets zero, and a second charge is only valid after a refund (e.g. reinstatement after a Void).

**Cancellation Fee (CAN)**:
A historical admin fee of €50 for some Cancellation reasons, capped at the refund due; never collected, only deducted from a refund. No longer charged (CPC regulation); may still appear with a zero amount.

**Collection Fee (CLF)**:
An admin fee for a failed monthly instalment, added on its resubmission. Implemented but never used outside the Payment Schedule Service's integration tests: compliance forbids charging it.

**Annualised Premium**:
The policy's full-year net premium, held on the policy rather than on any Schedule Item.

**Cover Start Date**:
The date cover begins; the reliable start of the cover period.
_Avoid_: Inception date (a legacy duplicate field)

**Admin Fee Item**:
A Schedule Item that carries admin fees. A derived category, not a Collection Type. Its period starts on the date the policy or change was sold, not on the cover period, so it may start before the cover start.

### Policy lifecycle

**Mid-Term Adjustment (MTA)**:
A change to a policy during its cover period. On an Annual schedule (already paid) it adds a Pro-Rata Item for the period the change is effective. On a Monthly schedule it adds Pro-Rata Items, if any, for the running and Buffer Periods already collected, and replaces future Full Items not yet processed.

**Cancellation**:
Ending a policy's cover early; the policy's status becomes CX and its cover end date moves to the cancellation date.

**Void**:
A Cancellation with a full refund, as if there was never cover: the schedule nets to zero, Stamp Duty included. Cover end equals cover start, but that alone doesn't make a Void — a Cancellation on the start date with a non-zero total still gave a day of cover.

**Surgery**:
A manual intervention by IT that edits a schedule's data directly to correct a wrongly collected schedule, outside the normal MTA flow. Marked by a person's email as the modifier instead of a system account (SYSTEM, RERATES), which system-generated schedules always carry, even for adviser sales and MTAs.
_Avoid_: Hotfix, manual fix

**Renewal**:
The start of a new year term, which gets a completely new schedule. The only point where a customer may change from Annual to Monthly collection.

### Collections

**Collection Transaction**:
One payment attempt recorded by the Collections Service against one or more Schedule Items.
_Avoid_: Collection

**Reconciliation**:
Matching Collection Transactions to the Schedule Items they paid, to establish each item's Reconciled Outcome.

**Reconciled Outcome**:
The actual result for a Schedule Item after Reconciliation: Collected, Rejected, Refunded or Pending, flagged as retried when a resubmission or real-time retry occurred. "Refunded" means money returned after collection, unrelated to a Refund Item.

### Anomalies

**Schedule Anomaly**:
A detected condition suggesting a schedule is unusual or possibly wrong, with a severity of info or warning.
_Avoid_: Error, invalid schedule

**Frequency Switch**:
A Schedule Anomaly (info) where a schedule changed from Monthly to Annual collection partway through its cover period. The reverse only happens at Renewal, so it never occurs within one schedule.

**Orphaned Pro-Rata Item**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): a Pro-Rata Item with no Basis Item.

**Nested Basis Item**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): a Basis Item that has a Basis Item of its own.

**Pro-Rata Amount Mismatch**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): a Pro-Rata Item whose amount, in a scenario the tool knows how to check, differs from the amount expected from its Basis Item beyond rounding.

**Stamp Duty Imbalance**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): a schedule whose Stamp Duty charges and refunds don't alternate, or don't net to one (live) or zero (Voided).

**Annual Premium Mismatch**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): an Annual schedule without Pro-Rata Items whose Full Item spanning the whole cover period has a net amount different from the Annualised Premium.

**Collection Fee Charged**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): a schedule carrying a Collection Fee of any amount, which compliance forbids.

**Prohibited Cancellation Fee**:
A Schedule Anomaly (warning): a Cancellation Fee with a non-zero amount on a Cancellation whose cover end date is after 30/06/2026, the latest date one may have been charged. A zero-amount Cancellation Fee is allowed.

**Unexpected Stamp Duty Amount**:
A Schedule Anomaly (info): a Stamp Duty charge other than the current fixed amount.

**Surgery Detected**:
A Schedule Anomaly (info): the schedule was last modified by a person rather than a system account, indicating a Surgery.

**Unverified Pro-Rata Amount**:
A Schedule Anomaly (info): a Pro-Rata Item whose scenario the tool cannot yet check, so its amount was not verified.
